## en-US (taban)

**Title:** Space Cat: Launch Terminal
**Short description (80):**
Live rocket launch countdowns on your wrist. Every agency, every mission.

**Full description:**
The mission control terminal for your wrist.

Space Cat tracks every orbital rocket launch — SpaceX, NASA, Rocket Lab, ULA, ESA, ISRO, CASC and more — and puts a live countdown exactly where you'll see it: your watch face, your tiles, your wrist.

🚀 LIVE TILE — A real ticking countdown, updated every second, with a 24-hour progress ring and GO/HOLD status. No frozen numbers.
⏱️ COMPLICATIONS — Add T-minus to any watch face: live text countdown or a filling progress arc.
📋 MISSION FILES — Vehicle, agency, orbit, pad, launch window and mission briefing for every flight.
🔔 SMART ALERTS — T-10 minutes, T-1 hour, GO/HOLD changes and delays. Each one optional. No spam: we only notify on real changes.
📰 THE WIRE — Latest spaceflight headlines with summaries, right on your wrist.
🛰️ STANDALONE — Works without your phone. Your watch syncs on its own.

Launch data by Launch Library 2, news by Spaceflight News API (The Space Devs).
Dedicated to Félicette — the only cat who ever flew to space.
