PRIVACY POLICY

Space Cat: Launch Terminal — app & website · effective 2026-08-01

Short version: we collect no personal data. No accounts, no ads, no tracking profiles and no analytics SDKs in the app.

THE APP (Wear OS & watchOS)

Space Cat: Launch Terminal runs entirely on your watch. It does not require an account, does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information, and contains no advertising or third-party tracking SDKs. Notification preferences and favorites are stored locally on your device only.

To show launch data, the app talks directly from your device to public, keyless APIs: Launch Library 2 (The Space Devs), Spaceflight News API, Open-Meteo and wheretheiss.at. These requests contain no personal data beyond what any internet request technically includes (your IP address, handled per those services' own policies).

THIS WEBSITE (spacecat.watch)

No cookies, no login and no forms. Space data is fetched either directly by your browser or through read-only, same-origin adapters that cache public source data. A launch you save via remind goes straight from your browser to your own calendar.

Terminal progress, recent public-data snapshots, training scores and discovery hints may be stored in your browser only. They are not sent back to us. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics — a cookieless service that provides aggregate traffic measurements without creating advertising profiles or storing identifiers on your device.
