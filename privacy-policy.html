SPACE CAT — PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: July 23, 2026

> TL;DR

Space Cat collects no personal data. None. Zero.

> What the app does

Space Cat: Launch Terminal is a watch app that displays rocket launch schedules and spaceflight news. To do this it fetches public data from:

· Launch Library 2 (thespacedevs.com) — launch schedules
· Spaceflight News API (spaceflightnewsapi.net) — news headlines

These are anonymous, read-only requests. No account, no login, no identifiers, no analytics, no ads, no tracking SDKs.

> Data stored on your device

Launch schedules and news are cached locally on your watch so the app works offline. This cache never leaves your device.

> Notifications

Launch alerts (T-10 min, GO/HOLD, delays) are generated locally on your device. No push servers are involved.
